# 📙 BP Company Policies & Code of Conduct

**Document ID**: BP-HR-CODE-2026  
**Version**: 5.0  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Scope**: All store associates, managers, and forecourt support staff.

---

## Section A: Business Integrity, Anti-Bribery & Gift Regulations

### A.1 Gift Acceptance Limits
BP is committed to maintaining high standards of integrity. Employees must ensure all business transactions are free from conflict of interest:
* **Gifts Limitation**: Employees must not accept any gifts, merchandise, entertainment, or financial favors from vendors, suppliers, or partners that exceed a nominal value of **$25**.
* **Escalation**: Any gift exceeding $25 must be formally declared to the compliance department.
* **Anti-Bribery Policy**: Zero tolerance for bribes, kickbacks, or facilitation payments, direct or indirect. Employees must refuse and report any such attempts.

### A.2 Relatives Disclosure
Employees must declare if close relatives work for direct retail competitors or active supply chain vendors:
* Conflict of interest declarations must be updated annually or within 30 days of changes.

---

## Section B: Customer Privacy, Data Protection & IT Safety

### B.1 GDPR & CCPA Compliance
Customer data collected through loyalty program sign-ups must be kept strictly confidential:
* **Storage**: Never write customer phone numbers or email addresses on scratch paper. All data must be entered directly into the secure loyalty terminal.
* **Sharing**: Personal data must not be shared with external third parties under any circumstances.

### B.2 POS Terminal Security
* **Terminal Lock**: Never leave a POS terminal logged in and unattended. 
* **Action**: Cashiers must lock their terminal screens when stepping away from the register.
* **Authentication**: Sharing passwords or logging in with another employee's credentials is cause for disciplinary action.

### B.3 Password Rules
* Passwords must be updated every **60 days**.
* Must contain at least 8 characters, including uppercase/lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
