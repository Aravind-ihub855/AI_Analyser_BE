## Chapter 1: Vendor Scorecard & KPIs

### 1.1 Vendor Performance Metrics
All active BP suppliers and logistics vendors are evaluated monthly using a weighted evaluation model:
1. **Fill Rate (40% weight)**: The percentage of ordered units successfully delivered.  
   $$\text{Fill Rate} = \frac{\text{Received Items} \times 100}{\text{Expected Items}}$$
2. **On-Time Delivery Rate (25% weight)**: Calculated based on deliveries completed within the scheduled window.  
   $$\text{On-Time Delivery Rate} = \frac{\text{On-Time Deliveries} \times 100}{\text{Total Deliveries}}$$
3. **Order Accuracy (20% weight)**: Measures the percentage of delivered units that pass inspection.  
   $$\text{Order Accuracy} = 100\% - \text{Rejection Rate}$$ (where Rejection Rate is rejected items / received items).
4. **Stockout Score (10% weight)**: Penalty points applied for delayed shipments causing product stockouts.  
   $$\text{Stockout Score} = \max(0, 100 - (\text{Delayed POs} \times 5))$$
5. **Lead Time Score (5% weight)**: Measures variance from the contracted lead time in days.  
   $$\text{Lead Time Score} = \max(0, 100 - (\text{Average Lead Time} \times 10))$$

### 1.2 Status Classification Tiers
* **Excellent (Score >= 95)**: Vendor is eligible for contract extensions, bulk ordering bonuses, and preferred partner status.
* **Good (85 <= Score < 95)**: Standard operating status. Maintain normal reordering routines.
* **Needs Improvement (75 <= Score < 85)**: Vendor is put on a 30-day corrective action plan. Mandatory weekly audits.
* **Critical (Score < 75)**: Triggers an automatic system escalation ticket. Contract review or potential termination.

## Chapter 2: Automatic Reordering Guidelines

### 2.1 Reorder Level (ROL)
The Reorder Level represents the stock inventory quantity that triggers a replenishment Purchase Order (PO). ROL is calculated as:
$$\text{ROL} = (\text{Average Daily Consumption} \times \text{Lead Time in Days}) + \text{Safety Stock Level}$$
* **Safety Stock Level Calculation**: Computed based on consumption variance and target service levels:
  $$\text{Safety Stock} = \lceil \text{Average Daily Consumption} \times 0.5 \times \text{Lead Time in Days} \rceil$$

### 2.2 Reorder Quantity (ROQ)
The Reorder Quantity represents the standard shipment volume generated by the system:
$$\text{ROQ} = \text{Storage Capacity} - \text{Current Stock Level}$$

### 2.3 PO Approval Workflow
Auto-generated POs are placed in a `"Pending"` status.
* **Manager Review**: The Store Manager must review and sign off on all POs within 24 hours.
* **Auto-Escalation**: If a pending PO remains unsigned for 48 hours, the system auto-escalates the order to regional management to avoid stockout risk.
