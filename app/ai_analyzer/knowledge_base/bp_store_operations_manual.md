**Document ID**: BP-OPS-MAN-2026  
**Version**: 4.2  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Scope**: All North American BP Convenience Stores & forecourt retail locations.

## Chapter 1: POS Operations & Cash Handling Guidelines

### 1.1 Till Cash Drawer Limits
To minimize retail theft exposure and secure store assets, cash register drawers must not contain more than **$200** at any time. 
* **Alert Trigger**: When cash in the register drawer exceeds $200, the POS system automatically triggers an on-screen prompt stating: `"Safe Drop Required"`.
* **Safe Drop Action**: The cashier must immediately bundle the excess cash, place it inside a drop envelope, insert it into the automated smart safe slot under their register, and log the receipt.

### 1.2 UV Bill Verification
All cashiers are strictly required to check high-denomination bills before placing them in the register:
* Check all **$50 and $100 currency notes** using the UV verification pen.
* If a note shows suspicious markings or fails the UV light test, cashiers must politely decline the note and report the incident immediately to the Shift Manager.

### 1.3 Mid-Shift Till Swaps
Cash register drawers are assigned to a single cashier. Till swaps mid-shift are strictly prohibited without a full **Cash Count Audit**.
* The supervisor must log in, print a Z-Report, count all cash/credit receipts, and sign off before a new cashier takes over.

## Chapter 2: Shift Handover & Daily Store Checklist

### 2.1 Daily Opening Checklist (6:00 AM)
1. **Forecourt Inspection**: Verify pump lanes are clean, visual status lights on dispensers are normal, and spill kits are fully stocked.
2. **Till Float baseline**: Count and confirm the register opening cash float of **$150**.
3. **Cafe Activation**: Power on the Wild Bean Cafe coffee machines, initiate the espresso steam rinse cycle, and set the pastry display cabinet temperature.
4. **Storage Chiller check**: Record temperature logs for all cold inventory storage (must be under 4.0°C).

### 2.2 Daily Closing Checklist (10:00 PM)
1. **Financial Settlement**: Run end-of-day POS cash reconciliation reports and deposit the final safe drops.
2. **Cafe Cleanliness**: Sanitize espresso steam wands, clean coffee grinding funnels, and wipe food prep tables.
3. **Forecourt Lockup**: Cordon off pump islands (if non-24h), turn on overnight exterior security lighting, and verify lock status of all external cages.
4. **Security Cameras**: Confirm recording status of all cameras on the monitoring console.

## Chapter 3: Customer Returns & Refund Regulations

### 3.1 Return Policy & Windows
* **General Return Window**: Unopened packaged goods and general merchandise in original condition may be returned within **14 days** of purchase if accompanied by the original receipt.
* **Non-Refundable Items**: Strictly no returns or refunds are permitted for:
  * Opened motor oil and engine fluids
  * Tobacco and nicotine products
  * Prepaid phone cards and lottery tickets
  * Chilled food or hot deli items

### 3.2 Defective Goods Policy
If a customer returns a defective automotive accessory or retail item:
* The cashier must inspect the product defect and log the return in the POS system.
* The customer is entitled to a **direct exchange** for the same item or a **full refund**.
* **Refund Method**: Refunds under $20 can be paid in cash. Refunds of $20 and above must be credited to the original payment card.
