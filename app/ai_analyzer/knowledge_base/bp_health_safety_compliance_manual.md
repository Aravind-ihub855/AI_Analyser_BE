## SOP 1: Forecourt Fuel Spill Emergency Response Procedures

### 1.1 Minor Fuel Spills (Under 5 Litres)
1. **Dispenser Cut-off**: Deactivate the dispenser immediately using the supervisor console.
2. **Absorbent Application**: Spread spill kit absorbent powder over the spill.
3. **Hazardous Disposal**: Sweep up the dry absorbent and place it inside the designated yellow hazardous waste bin.
4. **Storm Drains**: Do not wash or hose fuel into storm drains or soil.

### 1.2 Major Fuel Spills (5 Litres and Above)
1. **E-Stop Activation**: Immediately press the red Emergency Stop Button (E-Stop) to isolate all dispensers.
2. **Evacuation**: Clear all customers from the forecourt and cordon off the zone.
3. **Fire Extinguishers**: Stand by with Class B/C fire extinguishers.
4. **Hotlines**: Contact local emergency services (911) and the BP Safety Incident Hotline at **1-800-555-SAFE** immediately.

## SOP 2: Wild Bean Cafe Food Safety and Cold Chain Temperature Compliance

### 2.1 Cold Storage Temperature Limits
* **Target range**: Storage chillers for dairy products, fresh sandwiches, and raw items must be maintained between **1.0°C and 4.0°C**.
* **Daily Inspections**: Temperatures must be checked and logged manually 3 times daily.

### 2.2 Cold Chiller Failures & Mitigation
* **Warning Range (4.1°C to 5.0°C)**: Shift lead must inspect door seals and ventilation. Re-check temperature in 30 minutes.
* **Critical Range (Above 5.0°C)**: If the temperature exceeds 5.0°C for over 30 minutes:
  1. Relocate all chilled inventory to a backup walk-in cooler.
  2. If left in a critical chiller for over 1 hour, the products must be discarded.
  3. Dispatch a service technician immediately.

## SOP 3: Forecourt Hazard Compliance & Pump Safety Rules

### 3.1 Refueling Engine Off Rule
All vehicle engines must be turned off before refueling. Cashiers must monitor the pumps from the store window and halt dispensers if customers attempt to fuel with engines running.

### 3.2 Forecourt Mobile Phone Ban
Customer mobile phone usage is strictly prohibited in pump lanes to prevent potential static discharge ignition hazards. Signs are posted on dispensers. Cashiers must use the intercom to notify violating customers.

### 3.3 Fire Extinguisher Verification
Ensure at least two dry chemical Class B/C fire extinguishers are located at each pump island canopy pillar. Inspect pressure gauges monthly.
